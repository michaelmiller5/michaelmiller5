## Featured project

### [Hospital Price Transparency: Syracuse and Rochester, NY](https://github.com/michaelmiller5/hospital-price-transparency)
Cleaned about 4.5 GB of federally required hospital price files from six hospitals into one SQL database and compared negotiated insurer prices for 10 common procedures. Found MRI brain prices ranging from $315 to $7,957 across commercial contracts, about 25 times, and showed the insurer contract matters more than the hospital.

**[Interactive dashboard](https://michaelmiller5.github.io/hospital-price-transparency/dashboard/)** · [Recommendations memo](https://github.com/michaelmiller5/hospital-price-transparency/blob/main/docs/recommendations_memo.md)
